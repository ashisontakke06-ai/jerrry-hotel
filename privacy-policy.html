Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy
Prototype Notice: Jerry Hotel is an interactive luxury frontend web design demonstration. No personal financial information, real passwords, or credit card transactions are transmitted to or stored on external servers.

1. Commitment to Guest Confidentiality

At Jerry Hotel, we believe that discretion and privacy are foundational tenets of true luxury hospitality. We honor the confidentiality of all guests, dignitaries, and delegates who visit our digital portal and private estate.

2. Information Processed in Demonstration

In this client-side demonstration, guest names, dates, and simulated booking choices entered into our reservation wizard are stored only locally within your browser’s transient SessionStorage and LocalStorage to enable a seamless multi-page experience.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We utilize minimal browser storage solely to preserve your preferred aesthetic theme (Dark Mode / Light Mode) and demo booking references. No third-party behavioral advertising cookies or tracking scripts are deployed.

4. Future Backend Architecture Integration

When connected to a secure production backend, Jerry Hotel will enforce end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant tokenized payment gateways, and GDPR/CCPA data access rights.

5. Inquiries & Data Rights

For questions regarding guest privacy or to request data removal from your local storage, you may simply clear your browser cache or contact our Chief Concierge at concierge@jerryhotel.demo.
