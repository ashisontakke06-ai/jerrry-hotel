What are the check-in and check-out times?

+
Standard check-in begins at 2:00 PM, and check-out is at 12:00 PM (Noon). Early check-in from 10:00 AM or late check-out until 4:00 PM can be requested during booking or through our concierge, subject to suite availability.

Is airport chauffeur pickup included with my reservation?

+
Complimentary private chauffeur airport transfer in our Mercedes-Benz or BMW limousine fleet is included with all Executive Suite, Family Suite, and Presidential Suite stays. For Deluxe and Superior rooms, private transfers can be added as a bespoke enhancement during Step 4 of the booking process.

What is your reservation cancellation policy?

+
Standard flexible bookings enjoy complimentary cancellation up to 24 hours prior to scheduled arrival (2:00 PM local time). Specialized packages or holiday peak period stays may have custom cancellation windows, clearly noted on your confirmation statement.

What dining venues and breakfast hours are available?

+
Our Champagne Breakfast buffet is served daily at The Grand Palm from 6:30 AM to 10:30 AM. L’Amour Bistro operates all day from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM for Mediterranean dining and patisserie, while The Starlight Sky Lounge opens for evening mixology and tapas from 5:00 PM to 1:00 AM. 24/7 in-room dining is always at your command.

Are children and extra beds accommodated?

+
Children of all ages are welcome at Jerry Hotel. Children up to 11 years of age stay complimentary when utilizing existing bedding. Baby cots are provided on complimentary request. For adults requiring extra rollaway beds, a nominal surcharge is applicable in King rooms and suites.

Do you provide valet parking and electric vehicle charging?

+
Yes, our subterranean secure valet parking garage operates 24/7 with our compliments for resident guests. We feature four 150kW DC fast EV superchargers compatible with all major electric vehicle manufacturers.

What are the Royal Oasis Spa and Heated Pool policies?

+
The rooftop heated infinity pool is open daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM exclusively for resident hotel guests. The Royal Oasis Spa therapies operate from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM; advance booking with our spa concierge is recommended to secure your preferred treatment time.

How do I access the Jerry AI Concierge?

+
You can click the golden floating ✦ icon at the bottom right corner of any page at any time to open the Jerry Virtual Concierge prototype for instantaneous answers regarding breakfast, timings, pool access, and bookings!
