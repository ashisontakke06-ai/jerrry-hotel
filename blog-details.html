Gastronomy & Epicurean Craft

The Architecture of Flavours: Inside The Grand Palm Kitchen

Home / Journal / Article
By Executive Chef Antoine Moreau · October 14, 2026 · 5 Min Read

Before dawn breaks over the city, the prep stations at The Grand Palm hum with quiet intention. Copper pans gleam in the warm glow, while fresh baskets of Périgord black truffles and line-caught Breton seafood arrive in temperature-controlled transport.

Gastronomy at Jerry Hotel is never conceived as mere sustenance; it is an architectural narrative told through aroma, texture, and visual balance. "Each ingredient must have a reason to exist on the plate," notes Executive Chef Antoine Moreau, who earned his stars in Paris before leading Jerry Hotel’s culinary renaissance.

"A five-star dining experience should linger in the memory like a piece of timeless classical music—each movement deliberate, each note resonant."

The Pursuit of Rare Ingredients

Our autumn degustation highlights wild woodland mushrooms harvested by certified forages, accompanied by hand-churned cultured Normandy butter and 24-month aged Acquerello carnaroli rice. Our dedicated sommelier team conducts blind tastings each week to curate pairings from small biodynamic family estates alongside renowned Premier Cru Bordeaux châteaux.

A Feast for the Senses

Whether you are joining us for our legendary weekend Champagne Breakfast buffet or an intimate multi-course anniversary dinner, our team invites you to discover what makes dining at Jerry Hotel truly singular.

Antoine Moreau

Executive Chef & Director of Culinary Arts

Over two decades of three-star European Michelin experience, now steering the signature venues at Jerry Hotel.
