Cancellation & Refund Policy

Home / Cancellation Policy
Flexible Luxury: We understand that schedules change. Jerry Hotel offers transparent, generous cancellation terms designed around peace of mind.

1. Complimentary 24-Hour Cancellation

Unless explicitly indicated during your reservation, standard individual suite bookings may be cancelled or amended with zero penalty up to 24 hours prior to standard check-in time (2:00 PM local time on day of arrival).

2. How to Cancel a Reservation (Demo)

You can effortlessly cancel any demo booking by visiting My Bookings and clicking "Request Cancellation". The status will update instantly to "Cancelled (Demo)" without fees.

3. Late Cancellations & No-Shows

Cancellations received within 24 hours of arrival or non-arrivals without notice are subject to a fee equivalent to one night's room rate and applicable taxes.

4. Specialized Holiday Packages & Group Buyouts

Peak holiday periods (such as New Year's Eve or international festival weekends) and presidential floor buyouts operate under a 7-day cancellation schedule, clearly itemized prior to final confirmation.

5. Refund Processing Timelines

In a live operational system, refunds approved under flexible terms are returned to the original payment instrument within 5 to 7 business banking days.
